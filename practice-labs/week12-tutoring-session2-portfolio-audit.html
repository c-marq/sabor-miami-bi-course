⭐ OPTIONAL EXTENSION — The T005 Investigation

For students who finish the audit early. This is not required — it is an opportunity to practice detective work with a real data quality issue.

Your sales table contains 2 rows that reference truck_id = T005. But your trucks table only has trucks T001 through T004. Those 2 rows are orphan records — they point to a truck that does not exist.

Investigation Steps:

  1. Switch to Table View and click on the sales table
  2. Click the dropdown arrow on the truck_id column header to open the filter
  3. Uncheck everything except T005 and click OK
  4. Look at the filtered rows — what can you learn about these orphan transactions?

Clear the filter when you are done: Click the dropdown on truck_id again and select (Select All) to restore all rows.

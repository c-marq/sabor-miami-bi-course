Step 2 — The 5-Minute Presentation

🎤 Presentation Format

Both partners present from the same Power BI file, projected on screen. Each partner speaks. You do not need slides — the Power BI report IS your visual. Navigate through it as you talk.

Total time: 5 minutes. Your instructor will give a 1-minute warning.

Segment 3 · Challenges and Next Steps

~2 min
Who speaks: Both partners

This is the most important part. Be honest. Cover:

  • What was the hardest part of this project? Pick one specific challenge — not "everything was hard." Name the task, the tool, and the moment it felt stuck.
  • How did you overcome it? Did you re-read the textbook? Ask a classmate? Undo and try again? Trace the error message? Name the specific action that got you unstuck.
  • What would you do next with more time? More measures? Better visuals? A second dashboard page for a different audience? Time intelligence? RLS for different truck managers? This shows you are thinking beyond the assignment.

Step 3 — Portfolio Final Checklist

💡 USE THIS CHECKLIST BEFORE MONDAY Open your shared portfolio .pbix file and verify every item below. Check the box when confirmed. If anything is missing, fix it before the presentation.

✅ Portfolio Checklist

Data Preparation (from Labs 1 & 2)

  • All 5 Sabor Miami tables are loaded (or staging queries are properly disabled)
  • Null tips replaced with 0; null phone numbers left as null
  • Text inconsistencies cleaned (casing, typos, category standardization)
  • Dollar signs removed from menu_items price before type change
  • Date column correctly parsed (both formats resolved)
  • All column names are lowercase_underscore
  • Dimension data merged into sales (or proper star schema relationships active)

Data Model (from Week 12)

  • Star schema visible in Model View with active relationships
  • All relationships are Many-to-one (*:1), single direction, from fact to dimension
  • Date table exists with Year, Quarter, Month Name, Month Number, Day of Week, Day Number
  • Date table marked as Date table
  • Month Name sorted by Month Number

DAX Measures (from Lab 3)

  • At least 6 measures present (Total Sales, Total Tips, Average Ticket, Tip Percentage, Transaction Count, Unique Items Sold, or equivalents)
  • At least 1 CALCULATE measure (La Havana Express Sales or similar)
  • All measures show the calculator icon (not the column icon)

Optimization (from Lab 4)

  • Date Hierarchy present: Year → Quarter → Month Name
  • At least 3 unnecessary columns removed or hidden

Dashboard

  • Dashboard page exists with at least 4 visuals
  • At least 2 different visual types
  • At least 1 working slicer
  • Page has a title
  • Visuals are aligned and the layout looks intentional

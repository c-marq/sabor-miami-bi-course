What You Are Submitting

Your portfolio is the culmination of Labs 1–4, assembled into a single polished Power BI file and presented live to the class. You and your partner submit one shared .pbix file and deliver a 5-minute group presentation. Both partners receive the same grade.

File name: Team_LastName1_LastName2_Portfolio.pbix

Submit to: Canvas (one partner uploads the .pbix; both partners present in class)

1. Cleaned and Combined Dataset 30 pts

The foundation: data connected from source, profiled, cleaned, and combined. This is the work from Labs 1 and 2.

Excellent (25–30) All 5 Sabor Miami tables connected. Null tips replaced with 0, null phones left as null. Text inconsistencies resolved (casing, typos, category standardization). Dollar signs removed before type change. Mixed date formats correctly parsed. All columns lowercase_underscore. Dimension data merged or properly related. No duplicate rows.
Good (15–24) Data is connected and mostly clean. 1–2 cleaning steps missing or incomplete (e.g., a casing issue remains, or one date format was not parsed correctly). Merges are present but may have minor issues.
Needs Work (0–14) Significant cleaning steps missing. Data types not set correctly. Merges missing or broken. Column naming convention not applied.
2. Data Model with Relationships 25 pts

The star schema: fact table in the center, dimension tables around it, proper relationships. This is the work from Week 12.

Excellent (20–25) Star schema visible in Model View. All relationships are Many-to-one (*:1), single cross-filter direction, active. Date table exists with Year, Quarter, Month Name, Month Number, Day of Week columns. Date table marked as Date table. Month Name sorted by Month Number.
Good (12–19) Relationships exist and are mostly correct. Minor issues: one relationship may have wrong cardinality or direction, or the Date table is missing a column or the Sort by Column setting.
Needs Work (0–11) Relationships missing or broken. No Date table. Star schema not recognizable in Model View.
3. Measure Library 25 pts

The DAX measures that make the data talk. This is the work from Lab 3.

Excellent (20–25) At least 6 working measures present (Total Sales, Total Tips, Average Ticket, Tip Percentage, Transaction Count, Unique Items Sold, or equivalents). At least 1 CALCULATE measure that filters by a dimension column (e.g., La Havana Express Sales). All measures show the calculator icon. Measures produce correct, reasonable numbers when placed in visuals.
Good (12–19) 4–5 measures present and working. CALCULATE measure may be missing or returning incorrect results. Minor formula errors that produce slightly wrong numbers.
Needs Work (0–11) Fewer than 4 measures. Measures show errors. No CALCULATE measure attempted.
4. Model Optimization 20 pts

The polish: hierarchy, column removal, and evidence of Performance Analyzer use. This is the work from Lab 4.

Excellent (16–20) Date Hierarchy present with correct levels (Year → Quarter → Month Name). Drill-down works in at least one visual. At least 3 unnecessary columns removed or hidden. No essential columns (relationship keys, measure sources, sort-by targets) were removed.
Good (10–15) Hierarchy present but levels may be in wrong order or drill-down not verified. 1–2 columns removed. Minor issues with column removal choices.
Needs Work (0–9) No hierarchy. No columns removed. Or column removal broke the model.
5. Dashboard Page 25 pts

The visual layer: a report page that tells a story about Sabor Miami's business at a glance.

Excellent (20–25) Dashboard page has 4+ visuals using at least 2 different visual types. Includes a working slicer that filters all visuals. Layout is intentional: aligned edges, readable labels, a title text box. Visuals use the measures from the measure library. The page tells a coherent story about the business.
Good (12–19) 3 visuals present, or slicer is missing/non-functional. Layout is present but could be cleaner. Visuals work but the page does not tell a clear story.
Needs Work (0–11) Fewer than 3 visuals. No slicer. Visuals show errors. No dashboard page created.
6. Group Presentation 25 pts

The 5-minute live presentation where both partners walk the class through the portfolio.

Excellent (20–25) Both partners speak. Presentation covers all three segments: the data story (what was discovered and fixed), the model and insights (dashboard walkthrough with drill-down and slicer interaction), and challenges/next steps (specific challenges named with specific solutions, and a thoughtful "what we would do next"). Stays within 5 minutes. Presenters navigate the Power BI file live and speak to their own work with confidence.
Good (12–19) Both partners speak but coverage is uneven (one partner dominates). One of the three segments is missing or surface-level. Presenters read from notes rather than speaking naturally about their work. Slightly over or under time.
Needs Work (0–11) Only one partner speaks. Presentation is under 2 minutes or over 8 minutes. No live navigation of the Power BI file. Challenges/next steps segment is missing entirely.

📌 POLICIES
  • Group grade: Both partners receive the same grade on all 6 components.
  • Late submission: The .pbix file is due on Canvas by the end of Monday, Week 16. The 48-hour grace period applies (no penalty). After 48 hours: 10% per day, capped at 30% max reduction.
  • Presentation absence: If one partner is absent on presentation day, the other partner may present solo and both receive the presentation grade. If both partners are absent, a recorded walkthrough (2–3 minutes) may be submitted within 48 hours as an alternative.
  • Redo option: If a group scores below Good (90 pts), both partners may revise and resubmit the .pbix file once for up to the Good tier score. The presentation component cannot be redone.
